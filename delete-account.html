Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion for 2otbo5ly at any time. The same app account flow applies for both customers and chefs.

How to Request Deletion

  • Send an email from your registered account email address.
  • Include your full name and registered phone number.
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
Request Deletion by Email

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile details and account access.
  • Saved addresses and preferences.
  • Order and offer history linked to your identity, where legally allowed.

Data Retention

Certain records may be retained for fraud prevention, legal, tax, or payment compliance requirements. If retention is required, data is restricted and retained only as long as necessary by applicable law.
